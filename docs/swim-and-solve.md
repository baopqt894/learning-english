# Swim & solve

Register at the campus pool for a 25 m swim. Choose solo practice or create or join an online room. Each swimmer receives the same ten questions for the selected difficulty: four multiple choice, three listening and three word gaps.

Every correct answer earns 2.5 m. A swimmer with five correct answers reaches the halfway mark. A miss or timeout leaves the swimmer in place and resets the streak. After the initial ten attempts, missed items return in the order they were missed until the swimmer has answered every question correctly. A previously earned segment stays earned.

A streak shortens the time to swim the next segment and open the next question. The second consecutive correct answer swims at ×1.25 pace, the third at ×1.5, the fourth at ×1.75, and the fifth and later at ×2. The server records the finish time when the tenth segment reaches the wall. Bots follow the same distance and retry rules as human swimmers.

The solo swim leaderboard compares finishes under this ten-correct rule by difficulty. Records earned under earlier rules stay stored separately.
